This school is accredited

The school displaying this seal has earned NMAO accreditation — meeting nine independent standards of safety, integrity, and professionalism, verified by a national body rather than the school itself.

What this means for you

Choosing where you — or your child — train is a decision built on trust. Accreditation replaces “take our word for it” with independent proof: a school that has opened its doors to outside review and agreed to be held to a national standard.

The Accreditation Standards

Nine standards. Every one verified.

1

A Legal, Established School

A legitimate, accountable business with a clear mission — not a pop-up operation.

  • Registered business
  • Stated mission
  • Accountable ownership
2

Verified Lineage & Credentials

Instructor rank and lineage are traced and confirmed by the NMAO — never simply self-claimed.

  • Lineage traced
  • Certificates verified
  • Independently sourced
3

Safe & Insured

Carries liability insurance and maintains emergency readiness for everyone on the floor.

  • Liability insurance
  • CPR certified
  • First-aid ready
4

A Safe, Monitored Space

The training floor is supervised and equipped to protect students.

  • Video monitoring
  • Fire-safety equipment
5

Background-Checked Instructors

Everyone who teaches is screened and vetted, with clear standards for how rank is earned.

  • Background-checked staff
  • Vetted assistants
  • Clear promotion standards
6

Open & Transparent

A real, findable presence families can research before they ever walk in.

  • Public website
  • Social presence
  • Google profile
7

Professional Operations

Run like an institution — documented, fair, and protective of member data.

  • Operations manual
  • Fair discipline & appeals
  • Protected records
8

Clean & Health-Conscious

A hygienic training floor and equipment, maintained on a schedule.

  • Cleaning & disinfection schedule
  • Sanitized equipment
9

Held to Account

Accreditation is not a one-time trophy. It is maintained in good standing — and it can be revoked.

  • Ongoing good standing
  • Reviewed regularly
  • Revocable

Why the seal matters

Any school can hang a certificate on the wall. This seal is different: it is granted — and can be withdrawn — by the National Martial Arts Organization, an independent accreditation body. A school that displays it has chosen to be measured against a national standard, and to remain accountable to it.
