Choosing where you or your child train is a decision built on trust. Accreditation replaces "just take our word for it" with proof — reviewed by a national body, not the school itself.

The Accreditation Standards

Nine standards. Every one verified.

1

A Real, Established School

Not a pop-up. A legitimate, accountable business with a clear mission.

  • Registered business
  • Stated mission
  • Financially sound
2

Verified Lineage & Credentials

The instructors' rank and lineage are real — traced and confirmed, not self-claimed.

  • Instructor lineage traced
  • Certificates verified
  • Publicly sourced
3

Insured & Background-Checked

The people teaching your family are insured, screened, and ready for an emergency.

  • Liability insurance
  • Background-checked staff
  • CPR & first-aid
4

A Safe, Monitored Space

The training floor is watched over and equipped for safety.

  • Video monitoring
  • Fire-safety equipment
5

Qualified Instructors

Everyone on the mat is vetted, with clear standards for how rank is earned.

  • Vetted assistant instructors
  • Clear promotion standards
6

Open & Transparent

A real, findable presence you can research before you ever walk in.

  • Public website
  • Social presence
  • Google profile
7

Professional Operations

Run like a real institution — fair, documented, and protective of your data.

  • Operations manual
  • Fair discipline & appeals
  • Protected records
8

Clean & Health-Conscious

A hygienic floor and gear, with awareness for allergies and health.

  • Cleaning & disinfection schedule
  • Sanitized equipment
  • Allergy awareness
9

Held to Account

Accreditation isn't a one-time trophy — it's kept current, and it can be revoked.

  • Ongoing good standing
  • Reviewed regularly
  • Revocable

Why the seal matters

Any school can put a certificate on the wall. This seal is different: it's granted — and can be taken away — by the National Martial Arts Organization, an independent body. A school displaying it has chosen to be held to a national standard.
